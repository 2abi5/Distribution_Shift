**Scope risk.** The journal publishes work on how technology interacts with society, economy and governance. The
manuscript is framed as a technical evidence synthesis. Without a technology-society argument (who acts on these
findings, and what they mean for adoption, accountability and regulation of agricultural AI) it is at risk of desk
rejection. Better-fitting alternatives in the same publisher family: Computers and Electronics in Agriculture,
Smart Agricultural Technology, Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture.
